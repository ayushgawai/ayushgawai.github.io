Teaching a real robot arm to pick and place

A hand steers a leader SO-101 arm and a follower arm copies every joint. 50 teleoperated demos (~110k frames across two datasets) trained ACT and SmolVLA policies. ACT completed 46 of 50 physical pick-and-place trials at a fixed cube position, with ~18 ms local inference against ~251 ms for SmolVLA.
