featured · geospatial agent

LEO satellite coverage risk

A location can count as “served” by satellite broadband and still lose the signal to trees or terrain. Which of North Carolina's candidate locations are at risk?

  • Built: the data chain from canopy, elevation and land-cover rasters to a risk tier for every location, plus a Claude tool-use orchestrator over five deterministic tools (ingest, sample, score, validate, report).
  • Result: 4,674,905 locations scored, 100 of 100 counties covered, 0.0% missing environmental values, a 250-test suite.

Sentinel AI

Camera walls need someone watching every feed. Sentinel screens video on site and escalates only credible incidents.

Role: team of five; owned the video and frame-streaming backend, async event routing, alert triggers and incident logging.

Result: won the HP × SJSU Edge AI Hackathon; six-feed wall at 13–14 fps. Demo calls went to a teammate, never to real 911.

Rename one upstream field: which repos, dbt models, ML features and dashboards break before the merge?

Role: built with Manav Patel; coding agents test each downstream repo in its own checkout.

Result: renaming user_id to account_id broke six checks, produced three fix PRs, and the merge gate waited for the real owners.

CarbonShift

GPU training runs at one power level no matter how dirty or expensive the grid is right now.

Role: grid-signal polling and the arbitration loop that throttles, pauses or boosts training.

Result: about 43% less carbon and 37% lower cost in simulation; scenario results, not datacenter savings.

SO-101 arm, imitation learning

Teach a follower arm to pick up a cube and place it, learning from 50 teleoperated demonstrations.

Role: built with another SJSU research assistant: calibration, data collection, training and rollouts.

Result: ACT succeeded on 46 of 50 physical rollouts at a fixed cube position; ~18 ms local inference vs ~251 ms for SmolVLA.
