Winner · HP × SJSU

Winner, HP × SJSU Edge AI Hackathon

I won with Sentinel AI, a multi-camera safety system that runs entirely on an HP ZGX Nano sitting in the room. No video ever leaves the building.

I combined YOLO-based perception with a locally hosted Qwen vision-language model that reasons about what the cameras see, then raises real-time alerts. It showed that privacy-first AI can still be fast enough for live safety monitoring.

Publications

Three peer-reviewed research papers, all published in 2026.

  1. 1

    Measuring Visual Attention Across Concurrent and Sequential Visual Perception Tasks

    What we found: how often the eyes stop to fixate is the most sensitive eye-tracking signal for telling one kind of attention task from another.

    Research paper · Proceedings of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting, 2026 · with J. Chiu, A. Tran, S.-Y. Huang, G. Liu, Y. Yuan, Y. Luo

    Read the paper Conference poster
  2. 2

    AI-Enhanced Human-Machine Interface for Power Wheelchair Navigation: Effects of Communication Strategy and Vibrotactile Feedback Location

    What we found: when an AI wheelchair warns its driver about an obstacle, people are more satisfied feeling the vibration in the seatback than on the wrist.

    Research paper · Proceedings of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting, 2026 · with S. Tang, A. Shah, S.-Y. Huang, G. Liu, J. Xie, G. Huang

    Read the paper Conference poster
  3. 3

    Evaluating an AI-enhanced power wheelchair obstacle alert system: Effects of multimodal warning design on warning-response behavior

    What we found: warnings that tell the driver what to do get faster reactions than warnings that only say where the hazard is, and adding seatback vibration beats audio alone.

    Research paper · Transportation Research Part F: Traffic Psychology and Behaviour, Vol. 123, Article 103801, 2026 · with S. Tang, A. Shah, S.-Y. Huang, G. Liu, J. Xie, G. Huang
