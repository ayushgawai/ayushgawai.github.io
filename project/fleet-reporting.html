Zoox · Perception data-mining fleet · 2026

Fleet reporting + failure tripwire

Weekly Postgres snapshots of the whole miner fleet into immutable, versioned S3 artifacts, with event-driven failure alerts that cut discovery from days to minutes.

how it works

  • Snapshots: Postgres aggregation into immutable versioned JSON plus an S3 manifest, read by a zero-backend dashboard.
  • Alerts: fire on the miner run-state transition to FAILED — event-driven, opt-in per miner; the owner gets miner identity, run ID, state, timestamp and a deep link.
  • Q&A: a read-only Slack bot answers from published snapshots — no tools, fenced untrusted context, bounded lengths, Vault-managed credentials.
