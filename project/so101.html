SO-101 imitation learning

Six-joint leader/follower arms with Feetech STS3215 servos: 50 teleoperated demonstrations, ACT trained from scratch on a ResNet-18 backbone and SmolVLA fine-tuning, then physical pick-and-place rollouts with video-measured error.

the numbers

2.49 cmmean placement error among successes

18 vs 251 msACT vs SmolVLA local inference

how it works

  • Data: calibrated joint commands and synchronized RGB frames from paired cameras, with integrity validation, camera-dropout handling and resumable 50-trial rollout scripts.
  • Policies: ACT from scratch (30k and 60k step runs) and a fine-tuned SmolVLA (20k steps), both rolling out on the physical arm.
  • The debugging story: follower motor IDs reverted after power cycles and wrist-roll/gripper collided on one Feetech ID. Traced to an EEPROM write that was never re-locked — patched LeRobot's motor-bus setup to write Lock=1 after ID or baud changes, which persisted across restarts and unblocked calibration.
  • Evaluation: video-measured placement error, a failure taxonomy, and checkpoint-selection analysis across the two policy families.
