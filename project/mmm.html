Marketing measurement & budget optimization · 2026

MarketMIX, MMM + MTA budget optimizer

A unified marketing-measurement system: a Bayesian mix model and a survival multi-touch model over 16M journey events, joined by Shapley attribution and a constrained SLSQP optimizer that hands back a spendable budget plan.

the numbers

7%channel ROI error, vs 31% for last-touch

+14%incremental conversions at flat spend

16MCriteo journey events in the survival model

Shapleyattribution across channels, not one model's view

how it works

  • Mix model: Bayesian MMM in PyMC Marketing with geometric adstock and Hill saturation; geo-lift experiment priors and transfer learning seed the coefficients.
  • Attribution: a discrete-time survival MTA over 16M Criteo journey events in PySpark, plus Shapley-value channel attribution.
  • Fusion: Shapley merges the mix model's incrementality with the survival model's path evidence into one set of channel contributions.
  • Optimization: constrained SciPy SLSQP budget optimization respects channel minimums and total spend, so the output is a plan, not coefficients. Prophet handles seasonality and promotions.

the design calls

Two engines, one answer

Attribution never hinges on a single model's view of the world.

Constraints are first-class

The optimizer returns a spendable plan that respects minimums and total budget.
