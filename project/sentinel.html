Sentinel AI

A fully local, privacy-first multi-camera safety system on the HP ZGX Nano: cheap on every frame, and expensive reasoning only when an escalation is credible. Video never leaves the machine.

how it works

  • Perception: YOLO26s-pose person and pose detection with temporal tracking; per-track motion rules (weapon, fall, run, loitering, sudden change) and optional CLIP screening keep the cheap path cheap.
  • Reasoning: a 16-frame evidence bundle goes to locally served Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B FP8 for event classification — WEAPON, FIGHT, THEFT, RUN, MEDICAL or BENIGN — plus a person description. Score-based escalation instead of every-frame VLM calls.
  • Operations: an incident state machine (NEW → ALERTED → DISPATCH_PENDING → DISPATCHED → TRACKING → RESOLVED / DISMISSED), SQLite event history, and a guarded emergency-call workflow with local speech recognition, ElevenLabs TTS, SignalWire telephony, human confirmation, a kill switch and one dispatch per incident.
  • Surface: a live multi-camera wall, site map, incident history, timeline, call transcript and operator actions over REST, WebSocket and MJPEG.

the design calls

Cost-fidelity funnel

Cheap per-frame perception handles volume; the VLM handles the few ambiguous, high-impact events.

No cloud, on purpose

Video stays on the ZGX Nano — a privacy and latency choice, not a limitation.
