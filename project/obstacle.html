C++17 · camera + LiDAR fusion prototype

Real-time obstacle alert policy

A hardware-independent wheelchair obstacle-alert prototype: multithreaded camera/LiDAR fusion mapping boxes to bearings, and a distance-triggered STOP/CAUTION policy with hysteresis and confirming frames.

the numbers

<2.0 mCAUTION ring; STOP under 1.0 m

0.25 mhysteresis band on the trigger

2 / 5confirming frames to escalate / de-escalate

~66.8 msp95 capture-to-decision latency in the laptop simulator

how it works

  • Latest-value slots: a frame slot and a scan slot drop stale work instead of queueing it — perception can never outrun the policy.
  • Fusion: a detector thread and a fusion+alert thread map camera boxes onto LiDAR bearings, so a 'person' is a thing in space, not a pixel.
  • Policy: depth drives the trigger — STOP under 1.0 m, CAUTION under 2.0 m, with a 0.25 m hysteresis band, two confirming frames to escalate and five to de-escalate, and immediate STOP for very close obstacles.
  • The shipped repo is a hardware-independent simulator with scripted detector and LiDAR streams plus interfaces for Jetson YOLOv8/TensorRT and ROS 2 integration — the published ZED 2i/YOLOv10n study lives on the wheelchair project page.

the design calls

Drop, don't queue

Latest-value slots mean a slow detector can't feed the policy stale geometry.

Distance, not detection, is the trigger

A box is a hint; the inward depth crossing is the cue, with hysteresis so it can't flicker.
