RGB-D warehouse perception

Warehouse case perception

Detects cases in RGB-D warehouse frames and tracks them in 3D: YOLO/OpenCV detection, Kalman prediction, two-stage Hungarian assignment through occlusion, and depth back-projection to XYZ.

how it works

  • Detection: Ultralytics YOLO or OpenCV on RGB; depth lookup and pinhole back-projection give XYZ.
  • Tracking: Kalman prediction plus two-stage Hungarian assignment keeps tracks alive through occlusion; collision-zone checks flag risky states.
  • Robustness: illumination, blur and occlusion variants, plus checkerboard and extrinsic calibration; C++ NMS keeps detection fast.

the design calls

Two-stage assignment

Cheap matching first, Hungarian only where it matters — robust to occlusion.
