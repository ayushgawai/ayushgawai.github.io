Side project · motion forecasting

AV trajectory prediction

Constant-velocity, Kalman filter, CTRV, ridge regression and learned multimodal-anchor forecasters predict 3 s of motion from 2 s of noisy 10 Hz positions through lane changes, braking and turns, scored with multi-hypothesis metrics.

  • 5forecasters, physics to learned
  • 2 s → 3 sobserved → predicted
  • 10 Hznoisy position input
Code on GitHub
Technical deep dive
  • Input: 2 s of noisy 10 Hz positions per agent. Output: 3 s of future motion.
  • Physics baselines: constant velocity, a Kalman filter, and CTRV (constant turn rate and velocity) for curved paths.
  • Learned: ridge regression on the observed track, and a multimodal model over a set of anchor trajectories, so a lane change and a straight path can both stay alive.
  • Scenarios: lane changes, braking and turns, evaluated with multi-hypothesis metrics.
