CarbonShift · Devpost

GPU training that follows the grid

A control loop polls WattTime + CAISO carbon intensity and price every ~60 s and drives a GREEN / AMBER / RED state machine that sets A100 power limits or pauses training at a checkpoint. FastAPI exposes control and status; a WebSocket streams cost and carbon every ~2 s.

  • ~43%less carbon, simulated
  • ~37%lower cost, simulated
  • 60 sgrid poll

Technical deep dive

Control loop

  • Grid monitor on WattTime and CAISO data with a mock fallback, polled about every 60 s.
  • Eco-Pulse arbitration engine with GREEN, AMBER, RED and NORMAL states driving a GPU power controller (A100 power limits).
  • Pause and resume around a checkpoint manager, so a pause never loses training progress.
  • FastAPI control and status endpoints, a background metrics loop, WebSocket updates about every 2 s, cumulative cost and carbon reporting.

What the numbers are

The ~43% carbon and ~37% cost reductions are scenario results in simulation, not measured datacenter savings.

the design calls

Throttle before pausing

Power caps keep training moving; only RED stops it.

Checkpoint on every pause

A red pause is free to resume, no progress lost.
