Side project · synthetic AV scenario corpus

Scene Miner: rules vs vectors for rare events

Rule filters on driving metadata, embedding nearest neighbours from seed scenes, Rocchio relevance feedback and a hybrid rule + embedding re-ranker, compared at a fixed 300-result review budget over an IVF approximate-nearest-neighbour index (k-means cells, tunable nprobe).

  • 0.96hybrid mean precision (rules 0.35)
  • 0.959recall@100 at nprobe 16
  • ~19×exact-search throughput

Technical deep dive
  • Corpus: about 200,000 synthetic scene windows with 10,701 labeled rare events.
  • Mean precision of what a reviewer sees in the top 300: rules 0.35, embedding queries 0.55, Rocchio feedback 0.68, hybrid re-ranker 0.96.
  • IVF index: k-means coarse cells, search only the nprobe nearest cells. nprobe 16 kept recall@100 at 0.959 at about 19× exact-search throughput.
  • The offline version of the Zoox question: how much of what gets mined is worth a reviewer's time.
