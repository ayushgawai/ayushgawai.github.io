CarbonShift

A 60-second control loop polls CAISO grid price and carbon, maps them to GREEN, NORMAL, AMBER or RED, and sets the A100 power cap through NVML, from 250 W down to a checkpointed pause. In a realistic scenario profile it cut carbon intensity ~43% and energy cost ~37%.

Airflow lands BART and VTA GTFS every 6 h while a Kafka producer streams live departures into Snowflake LANDING. dbt builds route marts, and Snowflake ML forecasts demand 7 days ahead behind FastAPI and a React dashboard.

Multiplayer Agent Sessions

Every human and agent action is one versioned SessionEvent with a gapless seq, stored append-only in Postgres and fanned out over Redis and WebSocket. Catch-up, intent, merge arbitration and routing models (M1 to M4) share one /v1/models/{id}/predict endpoint.

SO-101 robot learning

Leader-to-follower teleop over Feetech STS3215 buses records 50 demos (~49.6k and ~60k frames). ACT trained from scratch on a ResNet-18 backbone and a fine-tuned SmolVLA run on the arm: ACT places the cube in 46 of 50 trials at one fixed position, ~18 ms vs ~251 ms per inference. Includes the EEPROM Lock=1 motor-ID fix.

Columnar query engine

A rule optimizer folds constants, pushes filters through projections and joins into scans, and prunes columns. Footer min/max stats then skip 63 of 80 row groups on a one-year filter, and 2,048-row batches with selection vectors run the rest. 1,500 random queries match a row-at-a-time oracle.

IoT Smart Garden

ESP32 soil-moisture, temperature and humidity readings travel over MQTT to Azure IoT Hub and Azure SQL. Python checks flag sensor faults before Power Automate sends alerts and Power BI reports device health.

AccessiMed

A CLI-driven accessibility tool that audits a live page with Playwright and axe-core against WCAG 2.1 A/AA, normalizes findings to severity and rule IDs, then writes a fix — a deterministic no-key patch or an LLM suggestion — and hands you the git diff to review. Every scan and fix is stored in SQLite, powering PDF reports, a dashboard, and a CI merge gate.

WikiRisk

Spark ingests 29+ GB of Wikipedia revision history and cross-validates a SparkML model on 11.16M rows in MLflow, then a live Structured Streaming job scores every Wikimedia edit as it lands. Scored events go to a Parquet archive and a SQLite + FastAPI serving layer. Held-out recall is 89.6% at 0.762 ROC AUC.

AssistAI — wheelchair obstacle alert

An onboard ZED 2i + YOLOv10n pipeline detects pedestrians, holds them with temporal tracking, and triggers on a 1 m depth crossing. The warning is multimodal — twelve vibrotactile tactors plus a speech or tone cue — and the study measures how fast a person actually reacts. Published in SAGE/HFES and Transportation Research Part F.

LEO satellite coverage risk

A geospatial agent scores 4.67M candidate LEO-satellite install sites for tree and terrain obstruction, sampling NLCD canopy and USGS 3DEP elevation at each. A Claude tool-use orchestrator drives five deterministic tools — Python does the raster math, file-based state keeps millions of rows out of the prompt — and lands 100 of 100 counties at 0.0% environmental NaNs.

Visual attention, measured

Pupil Labs gaze, fixation, blink and pupil streams are aligned per trial, cleaned, and turned into features across global, selective and sustained attention tasks. Statistics show fixation count is the strongest discriminator, and everything lands in a model-ready dataset plus gaze heatmaps and cross-discipline reports. Published in SAGE/HFES 2026.

Unified MMM + MTA measurement & budget optimizer

A Bayesian mix model (geometric adstock + Hill saturation) and a discrete-time survival multi-touch model over 16M Criteo journey events, joined by Shapley attribution, feed a constrained SLSQP budget optimizer. Channel ROI error lands within 7% — last-touch was around 31% — and the optimized plan delivered +14% incremental conversions at flat spend.
